# Demo script — about two minutes

Record this with the Android screen recorder (Chrome, the deployed HTTPS address, or the phone on the same Wi-Fi as a laptop running `npm run dev`). Hold the phone in portrait. Do the actions; don’t read the stage directions aloud. If you speak, one calm sentence at each cut is enough.

Have the sample house ready. If a Presage key is set and you want the live path, do **Take B** instead of the sample check-in. Do not claim the numbers are a diagnosis. If a number looks odd, say “that’s what the camera observed” and move on.

## 0:00 — Open

Open the site. Let the waking line pass if the host was asleep.

Say: “Nudge and Nod is a daily check-in. The person can answer with a picture, a few words, or just by sitting here.”

Point at the line that says it is not a medical device.

## 0:15 — The sample house

Tap **Look around the sample house**. The word and PIN are on the screen: maple, 2468.

Land on the house. June has not checked in today. Yesterday is described in a sentence. Mention Arthur only if he is on screen: “Arthur doesn’t have enough days yet, so we don’t call his usual.”

## 0:35 — Their own days

Open **See the days** for June. Point at the green band and at the one day with the clay mark.

Say: “Different means it doesn’t sit with her own recent check-ins. The page says how that line was drawn. It is not a healthy-or-not line.”

Tap the “How usual is decided” disclosure for one second, then go back.

## 0:55 — Agreement

Tap **Start June’s check-in**.

Choose **I am with June. They are here, and I am agreeing with them.**

Glance at the three setup lines: prop the phone, face the light, one person.

## 1:05 — Take A, no key or no time: the sample

Tap **Try the sample instead — camera stays off**.

Show the Sample ribbon and “Camera is off”. Wait until the pulse number appears (about twelve seconds into the sample). If you can let it run, breathing appears around half a minute. The ring moves unless the phone is set to reduce motion.

Tap **Good** or **Tired**. Tap **End sample**.

Say: “That one is made up, and it is not saved. The real one uses the camera.”

## 1:05 — Take B, live, if the key is set

Tap **Start the camera**. Allow Chrome when it asks.

Prop the phone so your face and the top of your chest are in the preview. The words **Camera is on** stay visible. Point at **Live Presage**.

Stay quiet and still. When a hint appears (“too dark”, “hold still”), follow it once so the hint is in the video.

When the pulse number replaces the dash, leave it on screen. Point at the line that says how many valid seconds have been counted, and at “Warming up” on breathing if it has not arrived. If you have time to reach half a minute, let breathing appear too. You do not need the full minute. The ring should be still until breathing is actually live.

Tap a picture. Tap **Stop**. The camera light should go off.

Read the sentence the app gives about the day. Do not add a medical meaning.

## 1:40 — The page you could hand someone

From June’s days, open **One-page summary**. Scroll enough to show the disclaimer and the table. You don’t have to open the print sheet unless it is quick.

## 1:50 — Close

Open **House**. Show the delete control and the line that telemetry is off. Don’t actually delete unless you are willing to tap **Restore sample days** afterwards.

End on the disclaimer: “If you are worried, contact a doctor or a person you trust.”

Stop the recording.

## If something goes wrong

- Host still waking: stay on the waking screen for five seconds, then pull to refresh. That wait is part of the free tier and is fine to show once.
- Camera permission denied: switch to Take A and say the sample is there for this reason.
- “Someone is already checking in”: the server can watch one person at a time. Stop the other sit, or use the sample.
- A live number never arrives: stop, and say the library did not get a steady picture. Do not invent a reading.
